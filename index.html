Discover the Idea

What is Gospel Socialism?

The economy was made for humanity, not humanity for the economy.

Gospel Socialism brings together the Christian social vision of the Social Gospel with structural analysis of economic power, democratic socialism and an empirical approach to economics.

The Gospel provides its moral foundation. Social analysis helps diagnose injustice. Democracy provides the means through which society may respond.

It takes questions of ownership, class, labour and economic power seriously while rejecting philosophical materialism, historical determinism and authoritarian socialism.

Neither the market nor the state is sacred. Economic institutions are tools whose legitimacy depends upon how well they serve human beings and the common good.

Our Foundation

Christianity cannot be concerned only with how individuals behave within society. It must also ask whether the institutions shaping society are just.

Feeding the hungry is an act of love. So is asking why hunger persists amid abundance.

Charity responds to suffering. Justice also asks what produced it.

The Twelve Principles

01
Human Dignity

Every person possesses worth beyond wealth, productivity, class or social position.

02
The Gospel Has Social Consequences

Love of neighbour applies to institutions as well as individuals.

03
Structural Sin

Institutions themselves can perpetuate domination and injustice.

04
Study Material Reality

Ownership, labour, class and economic power must be examined seriously.

05
Economic Democracy

Those affected by economic power should have meaningful participation in it.

06
Property Has Obligations

Greater economic power brings greater responsibility to the community.

07
Neither Market Nor State Is Sacred

Economic institutions are means, not ends.

08
Accountable Power

Power should be dispersed, democratic and subject to scrutiny.

09
Democracy and Liberty

Political freedom and pluralism are expressions of human dignity.

10
Justice Without Hatred

We seek transformation of unjust relationships, not dehumanization.

11
Evidence Before Dogma

Economic institutions should be tested against evidence and reformed when they fail.

12
No Politics Is Salvation

No market, state, party, class or economic system can substitute for the Kingdom of God.

“The Gospel determines our ends. Reason and evidence inform our means. Democracy restrains our power. Love tells us whom justice must serve.”

Our Conviction

We seek an economy that serves, a democracy that reaches into economic life, and a society in which power is accountable.

Gospel Socialism rejects both the rule of capital and the worship of the state. It seeks the democratic ordering of economic life toward human dignity, shared prosperity, responsible stewardship, solidarity and the common good.

Faith gives us reason to seek justice. Truth teaches us how. Love tells us whom it must serve.
